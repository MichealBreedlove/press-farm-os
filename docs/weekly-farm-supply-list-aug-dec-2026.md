# Weekly Farm Supply List — Aug–Dec 2026

What to bring from the farm each week for the rest of 2026, by month.

**Source:** Press Farm OS `deliveries` + `delivery_items` (financial source of truth), Aug 9 2025 – Aug 8 2026. Each 2026 month is projected from the same month of 2025 (seasonality-matched). Weekly qty = month total / delivery weeks in the month, rounded **up** to a whole unit.

**Exclusions (per Micheal):** deliveries under $5 (1 delivery) and line items under $5 (13 lines, $25.25 of $131,234.95 — negligible).

**Caveat:** 2026 volumes are running ~20–35% above 2025 — treat these as floors, not ceilings. Weekly qty is the combined total across all deliveries in the week (Thu/Sat/Mon), both restaurants.
