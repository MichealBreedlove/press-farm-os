Introducing Press Farm OS
Our new ordering system

Hi team,

We're retiring the Excel order sheets. Starting now, all produce, flower, micro, and event ordering for Press, Press Bar, Press Events, and Under-Study runs through Press Farm OS — a web app built specifically for our kitchens.

Here's everything you need to get started.

What it is

Press Farm OS is a website you log into from any device — laptop, tablet, or phone. It shows exactly what's available from the farm for each delivery, lets you place your order in a couple of minutes, and keeps a running history of everything you've ordered. No more guessing what's in season, no more lost spreadsheets, no more back-and-forth texts.

How to get set up

  1. Go to https://pressfarm.io in any web browser.
  2. Click “Create a chef account” and register with your own email.
  3. Bookmark it. If you'd like it to open like an app on your phone, you can optionally Add to Home Screen (Share › Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or the menu › Install app on Android).

Every chef makes their own individual account — please don't share logins. Your account is tied to you, so every order is signed with your name. That keeps things accountable for the whole team: we always know who placed what and when. Your login stays active for 30 days, and you'll see only the items that apply to your kitchen.

Delivery schedule

Regular deliveries run Thursday, Saturday, and Monday. Place your order the night before delivery. When ordering opens for a date, it shows up automatically the moment you log in.

The ordering workflow

  1. Log in — you land straight on the next open delivery date.
  2. Browse by category — Flowers, Micros & Leaves, Herbs & Leaves, Fruit & Veg, Kits, Family Meal. Items show live availability, and anything in short supply is tagged “Limited.”
  3. Set your quantities with the + / − buttons. Add a note for any special requests at the bottom.
  4. Submit Order — review your full order and delivery date on one screen.
  5. Confirm — you'll get a confirmation email, and the order goes straight to the farm.

Need to change something? As long as ordering is still open for that date, just reopen the order and update it — last save wins.

Need a day that isn't Thu / Sat / Mon?

At the top of the order form there's a button: “Order for a different date.” Tap it, pick any date on the calendar, and the regular order form opens for that day. Use this when your kitchen needs our normal produce on an off day — say a Tuesday catering gig. The farm gets a heads-up that it's an off-schedule date and harvests for it. This is just regular ordering on a different day.

Events (this is different)

The Events section is not the same as ordering on an off day. Events is a request-and-approval flow for special or advance needs — wine dinners, private parties, larger or unusual quantities you want to lock in ahead of time. You pick a “needed by” date, name the event, build your item list, and send it as a request. We review it against what the farm can actually harvest and email you once it's confirmed.

“Order for a different date” = regular items, a non-delivery day, goes straight through.

“Events” = a request for special / advance items that we confirm first.

The calendar

There's a Calendar tab in the app. It's a month-by-month look at what the farm is projecting to have available — harvest windows for field crops and microgreens, with the dates each item opens and closes. It's a planning tool: use it to see what's coming so you can plan menus ahead, and tap any day to jump straight to the order form for that date.

Order history

Every order you place is saved under Order History — delivery date, item count, status, and any shortages we flag after harvest. Since each account is your own, it's also your personal record of everything you've ordered.
